1. What Banter is

Banter is an app for car enthusiasts. It lets you add your cars, read the forums and communities you belong to in one feed, take part in Banter's own community, message other members, find club events, keep your car's history and ask Banter for summaries and facts. Some features are free; others are part of a paid level (see section 9). We may change, add or remove features over time.

Banter is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the forums, clubs, publications, vehicle makers or other sites it connects to, unless we say so.

Banter is an interactive computer service that hosts content provided by its members. Posts, replies, photos, comments, messages, profiles and event listings on Banter are written by members, not by us, and the member who writes something is responsible for it.

8. What Banter writes for you

Summaries, answers, facts, reply drafts and analyses marked "by Banter" are written by an AI service (Anthropic) after you have agreed to it in the app. They are offered only when you ask for them. They may be incomplete or wrong. Facts show their source where one exists, and a figure without a source is marked as such. Check anything important, especially figures about safety, maintenance, legality or value, against the original source or a qualified professional before you rely on it. A reply draft is only a suggestion: you edit and confirm it, and it becomes your post only if you post it.

The automated review of event listings and reported content ("Review by Banter", Community Rules) uses the same service to read the words of the listing or the reported content and answer whether it keeps to the rules. Nothing else about you is sent, the service keeps nothing, and a person decides whatever the review is not sure about.

An analysis of an oil report or other document does not replace the laboratory's own comments or the advice of a qualified mechanic.
