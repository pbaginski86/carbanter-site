Five kinds of enthusiast, one app

Banter started with cars and keeps every word of it. The same app now holds the other things people love to own and talk about, each with its own communities, its own words and its own plan. You choose what is in your garage; Banter shows only that.

  • Cars

    The Garage

    Your cars by VIN or by make, model and year, their forums and clubs, club meets, drives and track days, recalls from the U.S. road safety agency, and the car's own history.

  • Motorcycles

    The Garage, for riders

    Your bikes by VIN, the rider forums and owner clubs for each, the bike weeks and rallies of the year, and recalls. Daily Rider and Road Captain are the two levels.

  • RVs

    My Rigs

    Your motorhome, trailer or fifth wheel by VIN, the owner forums and clubs for its maker and chassis, the RV shows of the year, recalls, and the active weather alerts for your home state. Weekend Warrior and Full-Timer.

  • Boats

    The Dock

    Your boat by hull identification number, decoded on your phone, the owner forums and the forums for its type, the boat shows of the year, and the tides at your home port from NOAA. Weekend Skipper and Harbor Royalty.

  • Aircraft

    The Hangar

    Your aircraft by tail number, the type club and the pilot forums, the fly-ins of the year, and the latest weather report at your home airport from the FAA. Cross Country and Left Seat. Banter is not a flight-planning tool and says so on every card.

All five kinds are open in version 1.3. A kind can be paused from Banter's side if its communities or data need attention, and the app says so when that happens. Where a figure bears on safety on the water or in the air, Banter says that the owner's manual, a qualified technician and the Coast Guard's or the FAA's own publications have the final word.

What Banter does

Banter borrows the language of road signs. Every thread, event and notice carries a plate that says where it comes from, so you always know what you are reading.

  • Garage

    Start with what you own

    Add it once: a car, motorcycle or RV by its vehicle identification number, a boat by its hull number, an aircraft by its tail number, or by make, model and year. Banter checks the number, suggests the forums and clubs for it, and shows recall notices from the U.S. road safety agency where that agency covers the kind.

  • Feed

    One feed across your communities

    Connect the forums you already belong to. You sign in on each forum's own page, shown inside Banter, and Banter never sees your password. New threads from all of them arrive in one feed, each marked with its source and linked to the original, with headlines from the publications of your kind and new videos from well-known channels, which open on YouTube. Tap "Catch me up" for a short summary when you want one.

  • On Banter

    Banter's own community

    A place for each make where owners talk directly on Banter, plus private messages between Banter members. Every conversation offers Block and Report, and reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

  • Events

    Events and reminders

    Club events, meets and track days for what is in your garage, read from the calendars clubs publish, and the big gatherings of the year for each kind, from bike weeks to fly-ins and boat shows, listed from their organizers' pages. Tap "Remind me" and your phone reminds you the evening before. Each event links to the organizer's own page.

  • Facts

    Facts for Banter

    Figures for the car, bike, rig, boat or aircraft a thread is about, shown under the thread and ready to add to your reply. Each figure carries its source. A figure without a page behind it is marked so you can check it first.

  • History

    Its history

    Keep service, modifications, inspections, oil reports and performance runs in one record on your phone, for each thing you own. Banter reads the files that common performance meters export. Share a report, or hand the history to the next owner when you sell.

  • Easy View

    Easy to read and use

    Easy View offers larger text, larger buttons and fewer choices on each screen. Banter follows your phone's text size, and can read summaries aloud.

How Banter treats you and your communities

  • Nothing posts without you. Every reply needs your own confirmation, for that reply and that community.
  • Your passwords stay yours. You sign in on each site's own page. Banter never reads or stores a password.
  • Forum content stays on your phone. Banter's server keeps none of it.
  • Banter's writing is always labelled. Summaries, facts and drafts written by Banter are marked "by Banter" and appear only when you ask.
  • Every thread links back. The original site is always one tap away.
  • No advertising. Banter is paid for by its members, and your information is never sold.

Three levels, for each kind

Banter is free to use. Two monthly levels add more room in the garage and more of what Banter writes for you. Each kind has its own plan under its own names; a member who pays for one kind gets every other kind at the member rate, 30 percent off.

  • Free

    US$0

    • One car (or one bike, rig, boat or aircraft) in the garage
    • The feed, Banter's own community, events, reminders and messages
    • A limited number of summaries and fact lookups each week

  • Daily Driver

    US$5.99 a month

    • Up to three cars
    • For the other kinds: Daily Rider, Weekend Warrior, Weekend Skipper, Cross Country
    • Unlimited summaries and Facts for Banter
    • Ask Banter, and reply drafts where a community's rules allow them
    • Alerts for single threads and communities, and a daily summary

  • Road Royalty

    US$12.99 a month

    • Unlimited cars
    • For the other kinds: Road Captain, Full-Timer, Harbor Royalty, Left Seat
    • Everything in Daily Driver
    • Analysis by Banter of oil reports, time slips and performance files
    • The car's history report and hand-over to the next owner

Founding Member mark for good, for anyone who subscribes before November 30, 2026.

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play, monthly or yearly (the first level US$49.99 a year, the second US$109.99 a year), one plan per kind of item, and renew until you cancel in the store's settings. A member who holds a plan for one kind is offered every other kind at the member rate, 30 percent off, for as long as that plan runs. The app shows exactly what each level includes before you subscribe. See the subscription terms.
